# QA-A Architect report — 2026-09-01

Reviewer: QA-A (architecture, independent). Scope: massing, proportion, bay rhythm, materials and ground-floor treatment of the ~20 buildings that face Union Square, judged against `refs/*/`, the 34 viewpoint photos and `docs/recon/*.md` / `src/data/facades/*.json`.

Evidence: `qa/shots/qaa/` (34 viewpoints + 24 custom cameras), `qa/compare/qaa/` (33 side-by-side/overlay sheets). Captures are a snapshot of the scene at 22:41–23:05 on 2026-09-01; `FacadeSpec.ts`/`FacadeBuilder.ts` were being edited concurrently (a `signs[]` field appeared after the captures), so re-run the cameras quoted here after the fixes. Custom cameras are quoted below as `cam=name:x,y,z,heading,pitch,fov` (all captured with `node tools/qa/capture.mjs --out=qa/shots/qaa "--cam=..."`).

## Score: 4/10 (category: architectural fidelity of the square-facing frontage)

Massing and heights are broadly right (Westin E-plan + tower, Tiffany slab, Hyatt slab, Apple pavilion, Macy's block, Neiman box, Argonaut palazzo). What breaks the picture is (a) the Westin base not being built at all, (b) Saks rendered as a windowless white box, (c) a family of material/colour errors that make the whole west and north frontage read as one pale grey-green condo estate, and (d) floating rooftop props over Post St. Fixing the five blocking items below would move this to ~6.5/10 without any new engine features.

## Blocking issues (must fix)

1. **[severity: high] Westin base is built as a plain wall — every ground-floor feature is missing.** `cam=westin_base:-76,0.4,15,290,12,70`, `cam=westin_powell:-64,1.2,5,261,20,80`, vp05, vp14. From the Powell sidewalk the base is a blank plinth with one row of ~1 m squashed windows; no Victoria's Secret/Pandora bays, no round-arched mezzanine, no maroon dome awnings, no 4-column portico, no balcony/flags (photo `refs/west_powell/01_westin_front_2021.jpg`, vp14 photo). Cause (verify with `__twin`): `west_powell.json[0]` sets `heightM:9.4` (relative to `info.baseY = -2.6` → absolute top 6.8) while the engine's ground datum is the *highest* street-facing sidewalk (Post end ≈ +1.75). Upper height = 6.8 − (1.75 + 4.8) − 0.6 cornice ≈ 0 → `nFloors` rounds to 0/1, so `detailEdge` is skipped (plain wall) or produces one ~1 m squashed floor. Fix in `west_powell.json[0]`: `heightM: 9.4 → 13.0`; `groundH: 4.8 → 4.5`; `floorH: 4.2 → 4.0`; `cornice: "medium" → "none"` and `stringcourseAfterFloors: [] → [1]` (the belt is a string course, not a cornice); `masses[0].baseY: 9.4 → 13.0` and `masses[0].height: 50.6 → 47.0` (keeps the 60 m top). Then check `nFloors ≥ 1` on edges 2/3 and that the Victoria's Secret, Pandora, marquee and column extras appear.

2. **[severity: high] Westin material and window proportion.** `cam=westin_tele:-5,1.9,-5,261,10,42`, `cam=westin_plaza:-20,1.6,0,261,12,60`, vp05 overlay. Render: pale tan coursed texture, big white-framed 1.5×2.6 windows with black reveals on every face of every mass (pavilion flanks included), thin dark cornice line. Photo: dark grey-brown Colusa ashlar, small punched sash windows (~1.2×2.2, one per 3.65 m bay), stone dominating (~25 % glazing), light courts reading darker, a massive projecting green-copper cornice, attic frieze with small square windows, balconettes on floor 11. Fixes: (a) add a `colusa` WallMaterial in `src/materials/Library.ts` (ashlar RGB ≈ 143/138/120, normal map as sandstone) and use it for `wall`, `base.material` and `masses[0].wall`; stop-gap `granite_grey`. (b) `edges[*].window: "win_dh_stone_1.5x2.6" → "win_dh_stone_1.2x2.2"` and `masses[0].window` likewise. (c) `stringcourseAfterFloors` on the main spec → `[1, 10]` (masses inherit it → belt under the attic floor). (d) `masses[0].cornice:"heavy"` is the best available; a copper-coloured heavy cornice variant is an engine request (see gaps).

3. **[severity: high] Saks Fifth Avenue is a windowless white box with a square corner and open glazing.** vp10, vp28, `cam=saks:-58,3.3,-45,351,15,70`, `cam=beacon_crown:-40,2.8,-30,340,22,50`. Photo (`refs/north_post/union_square_looking_north_2018.jpg`, vp28): white precast with a row of large 2nd-floor windows, rows of ~1.8 m square punched windows (~10 across), a deep blank parapet band carrying SAKS FIFTH AVENUE, a 4-storey rounded NE corner with strip windows, papered ground floor. Fix in `west_powell.json[2]`: `edges[0].window: "none" → "win_punched_modern_1.8x2.0"`, `bayW: 6 → 4.8`, `floors: 4 → 3`, `heightM: 23.5`, add `masses:[{polygon:<outline offset 0>, baseY:23.5, height:3.5, wall:"plaster_white", window:"none", cornice:"none"}]` for the blank parapet band; add a chamfered corner mass (3 facets, 4 m each) at Post/Powell to approximate the curve; change the closed bays' `module` to `"wall"` with the `letters` sign kept, or add a `papered` storefront variant (engine gap).

4. **[severity: high] Rooftop utility boxes float in mid-air over Post St, Neiman's Stockton face and the Whittell.** `cam=saks`, `cam=beacon_crown`, `cam=tiffany2:-14,2.5,-20,351,25,60`, `cam=neiman_stockton2`, `cam=whittell` — 5–10 dark boxes hanging at 15–55 m in each frame. Cause: `src/world/Props.ts:141-144` adds `street/utility_box` for every building with area ≥ 400 m² and height ≥ 12 m at `info.topY`, scattered up to `sqrt(area)*0.25` m from the OSM centroid with no point-in-polygon test and no exclusion of façade-engine/hero buildings (the comment says "massing buildings only" but nothing filters). Engine-built buildings whose spec overrides height (Westin base 9.4 vs 60 m OSM; Neiman `floors:2`; Macy's; Saks) and concave footprints (Westin E-plan, Macy's L) put them in the air. Fix: skip `exclude`/hero ids and any building with an authored spec, and reject samples where `!pointInPolygon(x,z,footprint)`.

5. **[severity: high] Macy's Geary façade: spandrel rhythm, bow backing panels, and the 233 Geary double grid.** vp04, vp23, `cam=macys_bow:-8,-1.6,45,171,15,60`, `cam=lv233:32,-2.4,40,171,15,60`, ref `refs/south_geary/01_macys_geary_facade_2017.jpg`, `06_imagnin_building_2008.jpg`. (a) Wings: `win_curtain_3.0x3.6` modules in `metal_alu` produce heavy dark spandrels; set the wing masses (`south_geary.json[0].masses[1]` and `[3]`) to `wall:"glass_tint"`, `window:"none"` so the wall is one continuous glazed plane (mullion lines are lost, but the flush-glass reading is closer); better long-term: a `curtain_flush` material with a mullion grid texture. (b) Bow mass (`masses[2]`): `window:"win_curtain_3.0x3.6" → "none"` — the transparent `glass_tint` wall currently exposes the inset `window_back` panels as floating dark rectangles. (c) 233 Geary: main spec `edges[?edge:8].window: "win_dh_stone_1.5x2.6" → "none"` so only the marble mass (`masses[5]`) draws windows; set that mass `bayW: 6 → 5.8` and give it `floorH ≈ 4.6` (7 rows over 32 m) via a mass-level `floorH` (engine: masses currently inherit `spec.floorH` 5.2 → 6 rows). Add the entrance canopy now that `canopy_metal` is placed at `sidewalkAt(at)+3.4`: `extras:[{kind:"canopy_metal",edge:7,at:31}]` (3 units side by side if a count field is added).

## Engine gaps and closest approximations (for the façade engine owner)

- Curved bays / rounded corners (Saks NE corner, Elkan Gunst): not expressible → 3–5-facet chamfered `masses` polygons carrying the same window module and stringcourses.
- Dome / oculus / rotunda (Neiman's City of Paris dome, Curran oculi): not expressible → keep the glass prism sheer (`window:"none"` on a `glass_tint` mass) and light its interior; oculi as small square `win_punched` at attic level.
- Parapet / rooftop lettering (SAKS FIFTH AVENUE, TIFFANY & CO., macy's, Neiman script, Williams-Sonoma frieze): **now supported** — `FacadeSpec.signs[]` (`{brand|text, edge, at, y, widthM, heightM, color, bg, illuminated}`) was added to the engine during this review (after my captures, taken 22:41–23:05). Author these entries: `west_powell.json[2]` Saks: `{text:"SAKS FIFTH AVENUE", edge:<Post edge>, at:25, y:25, widthM:22, heightM:1.6, color:"#2a2a2a", bg:null}`; `north_post.json[2]` Tiffany: `{text:"TIFFANY & CO.", edge:3, at:11, y:40.5, widthM:9, heightM:1.0, color:"#3a3a3a", bg:null}`; `south_geary.json[0]` Macy's: `{brand:"macys", edge:7, at:31, y:33, widthM:12, heightM:2.5, illuminated:true}` and the marble box `{brand:"macys", edge:7, at:80, y:36, widthM:8, heightM:2}`; `east_stockton.json[1]` Neiman: `{text:"Neiman Marcus", edge:8, at:16, y:24, widthM:9, heightM:1.8, color:"#f4efe6", bg:null}`; `north_post.json[0]` frieze lettering is gone in 2026 (Chanel) — leave blank.
- Tall vertical blade signs (CHANCELLOR, HOTEL STRATFORD, STARLIGHT, CURRAN): `signType:"blade_vertical"` with `height` and `floor`.
- Cornice colour/size: the 2.5 m green-copper Westin cornice needs a `cornice_copper_2m` variant or a `trimMaterial` per spec; all trims should default to the wall material, not dark grey.
- Awnings above ground floor (Bourbon Steak domes, Tiffany tan awnings): `extras:{kind:"awning_dome"|"awning_flat", floor}`.
- Balconettes / extras on elevated masses (Westin floor 11): `mass()` drops `extras` for elevated masses — allow them.
- Hipped / mansard roofs (Whittell, Curran): `spec.roof` exists in `FacadeSpec` but `roof()` ignores it.
- Patterned cladding (Neiman harlequin), flush curtain wall (Macy's), dark-frame windows (Westin bronze sashes): new `WallMaterial`s `harlequin`, `curtain_flush`, and a per-spec window `frameColor`.
- Papered / hoarded storefronts (Saks 2025, 340 Post): a `storefront_papered` module or `papered:true` on a bay.
- Deep blank parapet bands (Saks): already possible with a `window:"none"` mass — use it.
- Window modules cannot be scaled up beyond native width (`sx` only shrinks), so wide single-bay fronts (340 Post) need a wide module.
