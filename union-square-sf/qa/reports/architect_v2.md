## Blocking issues (must fix)

1. **[severity: high — engine] Upper wall panels of façade-engine buildings disappear when the camera pitches up.** Repro: `cam=saks_front:-45,1.7,-33,340,24,55` (Saks: everything between the floor-2 glass strip and the parapet is missing — 450 Sutter and the Beacon Grand are visible *through* the building), `cam=tiffany_top:-14,2.5,-8,351,38,28` (top four rows of windows hang in the sky), `cam=westin_attic:-28,2,-6,272,42,32` (top four floors of every pavilion gone, cornice ring floating), `cam=lv233` (Macy's roof mass windows with no wall), vp28, vp10. Counter-evidence that the geometry exists: `cam=postpowell_air:-40,48,-15,330,-38,60` shows Saks fully walled with its roof. Probe of the live scene (`__twin`): 185 `facade:<cell>|<material>` chunks, all `frustumCulled:true`, bounding spheres computed from geometry and plausible (e.g. `facade:-1,-1|paint_white` centre (−39,19,−83) r 34), materials opaque with `depthWrite:true`, `side:0` — so this is not ordinary frustum culling, a transparent material, or winding. The cut is view-dependent and only affects wall panels (windows/trims of the same floors survive), which points at a **material clipping plane or shader discard on the wall materials evaluated in view space** — most likely introduced with the slope-aware ground bands or the coplanar-'none' inset. Fix suggestion for the engine owner: temporarily set `renderer.localClippingEnabled=false` / strip `material.clippingPlanes` and `onBeforeCompile` injections from the wall materials in `src/materials/Library.ts`, re-shoot `saks_front`; if the walls return, convert the plane to world space (or clip only the ground-band panels). Until this is fixed every plaza-level view of Saks, Tiffany, the Westin and Macy's is architecturally wrong regardless of the specs.

2. **[severity: high — engine] Rooftop boxes still float in mid-air.** `cam=saks`, `cam=beacon_crown`, `cam=chancellor2`, `cam=argonaut`, `cam=tiffany2`, `cam=post340`, `cam=whittell`, vp05 (box at right over Post St), `cam=westin_plaza`/`westin_tele` (box at right). Dark 2–3 m boxes with a louvre face (`utilBig` scale range) at 28–40 m over Post St between Powell and Stockton, one in front of Tiffany's face at ~34 m, one ~10 m above the Whittell, one ~10 m above the Argonaut cornice. `Props.ts:143` now skips `detailedIds` and `:146` rejects samples outside `info.footprint`, and Saks/Tiffany/Neiman/Whittell *are* in `detailedIds` — so these come from other `BuildingInfo`s whose footprint overlaps these blocks (OSM `building:part` ways such as the Westin tower part `way/435582543`, the E-plan part, roof/shed outlines) or from the façade engine's own `rooftop:["penthouse"]` kit placed at `info.topY` rather than the rendered top. From `postpowell_air` no box sits on the Saks or Argonaut roofs, confirming the Y is wrong, not just the XZ. Fix: in `Props.ts` skip any info that is a `building:part`/`roof` or whose id is referenced as a mass/part by a façade spec, and place at `max(info.topY, specTop)`; in the façade builder place `penthouse` at the spec top and inside the mass polygon.

3. **[severity: high] Macy's rooftop `macy's` sits mid-bow behind the glass.** `cam=macys_bow:-8,-1.6,45,171,15,60`, vp04 overlay (real sign above the parapet). `south_geary.json[0].signs[0]`: `y: 32.6 → 40.0` (bow mass top is 10.9+27.5 = 38.4), `widthM: 9 → 12`, `heightM: 2.2 → 2.6`, keep `illuminated:true`; if `signs[]` supports a normal offset, push it 2.2 m outward so it clears the bow. Add the three flagpoles as `extras:[{kind:"flag",edge:7,at:25},{…at:31},{…at:37}]` if flags can be given a `y`.

4. **[severity: high] 233 Geary double grid persists.** `cam=lv233:32,-2.4,40,171,15,60`, vp08. Large `win_dh_stone_1.5x2.6` windows overlap the marble box's 2.2 m squares on floors 2–3; a further row floats above the box. `south_geary.json[0]`: `edges[1]` (edge 8) `window: "win_dh_stone_1.5x2.6" → "none"` for the run behind the marble mass (or restrict with `from/to` if supported), and set `masses[5].bayW: 6 → 5.8`.
