# Nintendo SAN FRANCISCO — reconstruction spec

Data file: `src/data/recon/nintendo.json`. Photos: `refs/nintendo/` (see SOURCES.md there). Researched 2026-09-01.

## 1. Location (confidence: high)

- **331 Powell St** — ground floor + basement of the 1904 Westin St. Francis landmark block, at the **NW corner of Powell & Geary**. Faces east onto Powell / Union Square plaza and south onto Geary.
- OSM store node 37.787487, -122.408455; Powell entrance node 37.787517, -122.408397. Victoria's Secret (335 Powell) is the next unit north; the Westin's columned lobby portico is mid-block; Bourbon Steak occupies the 2nd floor above the store.
- Former tenant BCBG Max Azria (vacated 2017; FILA earlier per NBC). Nintendo permit filed April 2024, opened 15 May 2025.
- Frontage: listing says **114 ft (34.7 m) wraparound**. Photo bay counts suggest ~3 bays on Powell (≈14 m) and ~4 bays on Geary (≈21 m) — *low confidence split*.
- Area: 9,000–11,000 sq ft reported; use **10,989 sq ft ≈ 1,020 m²** over **2 levels** (ground + windowless lower level).

## 2. Exterior (confidence: high, verified in refs 01–03, 06)

- Stone façade, rusticated ground floor, round-arched 2nd-floor windows with carved keystones and Bourbon Steak's burgundy dome awnings. Nintendo changed nothing structural: new brass-framed glass doors + sidelights at both entrances, internally lit fascia logos, character window vinyls, lights under the *existing* flat red awnings.
- Fascia sign: white "Nintendo" racetrack logo on red (#E60012) rounded box, ≈2.4 × 1.07 m (permit: 42 in high), bottom ≈3.3 m up, centred over each entrance. Second red square panel "Nintendo SAN FRANCISCO" (≈1.2 m) stands inside the Powell window north of the door. No blade or corner sign.
- Window vinyls: mascots marching toward the doors (Mario, Luigi, Peach, Toad, Yoshi, DK, Link, Isabelle, Inkling, Pikmin, Kirby, Pikachu). Same art on the SF-exclusive merch and 2025 cable-car ads.

### Materials / lighting (confidence: low)
White walls with franchise murals as zone backdrops, white gondola shelving + light-wood tables, light hard floor, white ceiling with track spots and tunable LED panels; "clean, modern, simple" like Nintendo TOKYO. Accent red #E60012 everywhere (signage, till, bags). Lower level: dimmer, projection-lit.

Statue heights for modelling: Mario ≈1.55 m, Link ≈1.8 m, Inkling Girl ≈1.5 m, Isabelle ≈1.1 m with Timmy/Tommy ≈0.8 m, Pikachu on Poké Ball ≈1.2 m total (all est. from "human-sized" descriptions).

## 4. Interactive display ideas (each grounded in a real fixture)

1. **Game wall** (L-1): walk up to the projection wall to start a 4-player Mario Kart demo; idle state plays a Nintendo Direct loop. Timed "sign-up slot" queue like the real store.
2. **Switch 2 demo kiosks** (L0 + L-1): pick a demo that matches the 2025-26 rotation (Mario Kart World → DK Bananza → Metroid Prime 4 → Mario Tennis Fever → SMB Wonder+Bellabel Park → Splatoon Raiders).
3. **Custom Switch OLED station** (L0 rear-right): tablet configurator for Joy-Con / strap / dock colours; the finished console appears at the front desk.
4. **My Nintendo kiosk** (L-1): "scan" to collect one of 18 mascot stickers; persistent sticker book.
5. **Pikmin scavenger hunt**: 10–20 mini Pikmin hidden across both floors; counter in HUD.
6. **amiibo wall**: hover reveals figure name + "rare" tag (Sora, Sephiroth, Little Mac…).
7. **Statue photo spots**: Mario at the Powell door, Isabelle/Timmy/Tommy, Link, Inkling Girl, Pikachu — selfie framing + shutter.
8. **Warp Pipe Pass gate**: on event days the Powell door checks a QR; queue barriers along Powell/Geary as on opening day (refs 02).
9. **Window parade**: at night the marching mascots on the glass animate toward the doors; interior spill lights the brick sidewalk.
10. **Stamp passport**: kid-mode collect-a-stamp at each franchise zone.
11. **Event calendar kiosk**: replay real 2025-26 events (Switch 2 launch 4 Jun 2025, Mario 40th 20-21 Sep 2025, MAR10 Day 2026, Splatoon Raiders 23 Jul 2026).
