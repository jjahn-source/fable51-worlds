Scope: Powell St, O'Farrell → Sutter. West side in detail; east side as sightline framing.

## 1. Westin St. Francis — 301-345 Powell (Geary → Post), west side of the square

**Facts (high confidence):** Bliss & Faville; two 12-storey south wings opened 1904-03-21,
gutted 1906, steel frame reused, reopened late 1907; north "double-width" wing 1913.
Façade = grey Colusa sandstone (MFA CAMEO lists the St. Francis as a Colusa example);
copper cornice (green patina); enamelled facing brick in the light courts. Landmark
building 60.35 m. Tower (William Pereira, 1972): 32 storeys, 120 m, beige precast with
bronze glass, five exterior glass elevators on the east (square-facing) face.

**Massing to model (medium confidence, from photo refs 01/06/14):** the Powell elevation is an
E-plan with the three wings projecting TOWARD Powell above a continuous 2-storey rusticated
base. From the square you read: `S pavilion (5 bays) | recessed court (3) | centre pavilion (5) |
court (3) | N pavilion (7)` ≈ 23 bays × 3.65 m = 84 m. Courts are set back ~4-5 m and read
darker. Storeys: ground 6 m + mezzanine 4 m base (10 m), then 12 guest floors at ~4.2 m
(floor 11 has balconettes; the top floor sits inside a carved frieze band with small square
windows), then a 2.5 m projecting cornice. Total ≈ 60 m. The tower stands behind the
north/centre pavilions, its east face ~25 m west of the Powell face.

**Ground floor along Powell (S → N):**
- 331 Powell, corner Geary: **Nintendo SAN FRANCISCO** (opened 2025-05-15, two levels,
  ~9,000-11,000 sf, wraps corner onto Geary; red Nintendo logotype). Façade context only.
- c.333: bays between Nintendo and the entrance — **UNRESOLVED**.
- 335 (centre, ~36-48 m from Geary): **hotel entrance**: three round arches with dark-red
  canopies, taxi zone, balustraded balcony above with US flag + red St. Francis crest flag
  (Pride flag in June). Inside: lobby, Caruso's espresso, Oak Room, Bourbon Steak (Mina).
- 335 north of entrance: **Victoria's Secret** (maroon dome awnings; OSM 2026-03 open).
- 345, corner Post: **Pandora** (OSM 2026-03).
- Post side: 427 Post — **vacant, 735 sf for lease** (Cushman & Wakefield); ex bike rental.
- Geary side west of Nintendo: **UNRESOLVED**.
Sidewalk: ornate double-globe lamp standards, maroon dome awnings over most arched bays.

## 5. Modelling notes
- Colours: Westin stone #8f8a78 (courts #6f6b5f), cornice #5f8a6a, awnings #7a1f2b, tower
  #d8cfbc/#2b2f33; Beacon Grand brick #c9b48e / terra cotta #e6dfd0; Saks #b9b6ae; Elkan Gunst
  #d9d2c3; Argonaut #b58a6a.
- Powell carries two cable-car tracks; the Westin taxi zone and Beacon Grand taxi stand are
  curb-side. (Transit itself is out of scope.)
- Sightline anchors from the square: Westin E-plan pavilions + tower; Beacon Grand crown at
  Sutter; Saks's blank rounded corner at Post; Elkan Gunst's terra-cotta corner at Geary.
