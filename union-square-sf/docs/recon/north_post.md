## Per-building specs (highest-priority block first)

### Apple Union Square — 300 Post St (NW corner Post & Stockton) — confidence medium-high
- Pavilion centroid 37.788682, -122.407194 (OSM). Foster + Partners, opened 2016-05-21, $50M; replaced 4-story Levi's store.
- Footprint: glass pavilion 34.0 m (Post) × 25.3 m (Stockton); plus 6.7 m wide × 3-level solid service wing on the west lot line (OSM 779012330) → 40.7 m total Post frontage. Lot ≈ 41 × 42 m incl. plaza.
- Height ≈ 14.5 m (OSM tag 15; 42-ft doors + fascia). 2 sales levels; roof with 130 PV panels.
- Post face: fully glazed; two steel-framed sliding glass panels 42 ft (12.8 m) tall (Chronicle/ArchDaily 2013 revision quoted 44 ft × 23 ft each). Stockton face of pavilion: solid grey brushed-metal panel wall, no glazing (photo 2018). Rear/plaza (north) face: glass wall at plaza level.
- Plaza (north of pavilion, at the store's upper level): ≈ 34 m E-W × 17 m N-S between pavilion and the Grand Hyatt's south flank; hedged planters, trees in white round planters, tables. Ruth Asawa "San Francisco Fountain" (1973 bronze cylinder ~3 m dia.) at the EAST end at the head of the steps down to Stockton St (~2 m drop, est.). Green wall (creeping fig) covers the Hyatt flank the full plaza length, ~15 m (50 ft) tall, with a vertical waterfall strip (Dezeen says at the western end; 2018 photo shows a dark strip ~2/3 along).
- Colors: glass #9fb2bd, metal #6c6f72, stone wing #cfcbc3.

### 340 Post — Bullock & Jones Building (Williams-Sonoma 2003–Jan 2026; Chanel) — medium
- 1923, Reid Bros.; 3 floors, 17 m; only 11.8 m wide × 41.8 m deep; 18,000 sq ft. Cream classical facade, single wide bay, ornate bracketed cornice (frieze once lettered WILLIAMS-SONOMA). Chanel bought for $63M (Dec 2021); W-S closed end of Jan 2026; Chanel fit-out (skylight atrium, 3rd-floor VIP gallery), opening date UNRESOLVED → model as boarded/under-renovation storefront in 2026.

### Tiffany Building — 350-360 Post — high
- 1972, SOM ("background building between two Classical buildings"; a.k.a. Qantas Building). 11 floors, 42 m (140-ft limit), 22.4 m wide × 40.5 m deep, 100,000 sq ft. Light beige-grey precast grid, 7 square punched windows per floor, parapet sign "TIFFANY & CO.". Two-story light-grey granite base; entrance is a polished red-brown granite Art Deco stepped portal with stainless paneled door and stainless letters; tan awnings over flanking small windows. NOT a columned classical front. Tiffany store on 2 floors (17,000 sq ft), open 2026.

### Saks Fifth Avenue building — 384-398 Post (NE corner Post & Powell) — high
- 1981 (built 1980), HOK. 5 floors + basement, ~27 m, 135,000 sq ft, 163 ft (49.7 m) Post frontage (OSM 51.2) × 41.7 m on Powell. White/cream precast; rounded corner at Post/Powell with strip windows; upper floors rows of ~1.8 m square punched windows (≈10 across on Post); deep smooth parapet band with SAKS FIFTH AVENUE letters (removal not confirmed); continuous ground-floor canopy over glazed storefronts. Store went appointment-only 2024-08-28, CLOSED 2025-05-10; lease to early 2027; CBRE marketing the building. Owner Lincoln Property Co./Cara Investment.

### 278 Post — Nike San Francisco (NE corner Post & Stockton) — low-medium
