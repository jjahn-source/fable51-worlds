Companion to `src/data/recon/streets.json` (the authoritative, coordinate-bearing deliverable).
All widths are metres. Right-of-way (ROW) for every downtown grid street here is 68.75 ft = **20.96 m**;
curb-to-curb / sidewalk splits below are *derived* (OSM sidewalk & track offsets, lane counts, the published
21-ft Powell sidewalk) and should be treated as ±1 m. Confidence tags: H / M / L.

## 3. Cable cars (Powell–Mason & Powell–Hyde)
- Both lines share Powell from the Market turntable to Jackson; NB track east, SB track west; gauge 1,067 mm; cable 9.5 mph (15.3 km/h); 7 a.m.–11 p.m., 9–20 min headways.
- From Geary north the two track lanes are painted red with white "MUNI ONLY" stencils (photo-verified 2017; M that it persists); Market–Geary the tracks sit in a brick pedestrian-priority roadway.
- Stops (all sidewalk stops, sign only, unless noted): O'Farrell (NB E side S of O'Farrell / SB W side N of it); **Geary** (NB 37.787239,-122.408143 E side S of Geary; SB 37.787463,-122.408342 W side N of Geary, "Union Square"); **Post** (NB = raised boarding island 37.788138,-122.408356 east of the NB track, rebuilt 2025-26; SB 37.788398,-122.408541 W side N of Post); Sutter (both S of Sutter); Bush.
- Cars: single-ended, 8.4 × 2.44 m, ~3.1 m high, 7.0 t, 60 pax (29 seated), fleet 1–28 (27 in service). Livery maroon + cream with gold-leaf lettering; several heritage green/cream cars (1, 26). Brass bell at roof front, centre headlight, "DO NOT PASS CABLE CAR" rear sign.

## 4. Signals
All 16 corner/boundary intersections are signalised (OSM, several field-surveyed); Maiden Lane crossings are unsignalised. Post-mounted 3-section heads on black steel poles (occasional short mast arm), pedestrian countdown heads everywhere (H), APS push-buttons added on Powell at Ellis/O'Farrell/Geary/Post 2025-26. Coordinated ~60 s cycle (L). Key restrictions: SB Powell must turn right at Geary; no left from WB Geary onto Powell; EB O'Farrell/NB Powell entry to the promenade only for Muni/taxi/commercial; right-turn-only outer lanes on Post and O'Farrell at Stockton and on Geary at Powell.

## 6. Station, garages
- Union Square/Market St Station (T Third, opened 2022-11-19): main headhouse at the plaza's SE corner, 37.787657,-122.407145 (escalators + stairs) with a glass elevator at 37.787665,-122.407057; second entrance at Stockton & Ellis (outside boundary); concourse to Powell St BART/Muni.
- Union Square Garage (333 Post, 670 spaces): Geary N-curb 2-lane entrance (E driveway, 37.787716,-122.407391; 6'7"/8' clearances) and exit (W driveway, 37.787696,-122.407555); Post S-curb entrance (W driveway, 37.788147,-122.407727) and exit (E driveway, 37.788188,-122.407397), 6'7". Pedestrian stairs/elevators within the plaza (positions L).
- Sutter-Stockton Garage (444 Stockton, 1,865 spaces): driveways on the E side of Stockton 35–42 m N of Sutter plus a Bush St entrance and an exit bridge to Bush — just outside the boundary.

## 7. Furniture
- **Streetlights**: historic 1917-18 ornamental standards — dark green-black cast-iron fluted poles with gilded collar and a single acorn/urn opal lantern (~4.5 m pole, lantern top ~5.2 m), ~15–20 m apart — on Powell and the square frontages (design photo-verified; name "Golden Triangle" unverified); plus black SFPUC standard poles (6.7–9.1 m mounting, LED 3000 K cobra/teardrop) ~28–30 m apart. Union Square Alliance banners on poles; Powell trees wrapped in white string lights.
- **Trash**: SF green "Renaissance" cans (1.2 m, domed lid) still standard; "Slim Silhouette" replacement funded 2025. **Meters**: grey IPS smart meters on Mason/Sutter/O'Farrell/Grant only; square frontages are bus/taxi/loading/no-stopping curbs. **Hydrants**: white barrels, blue bonnets on the domestic hydrants around Post/Stockton/Sutter; AWSS hydrants black-topped (lower zone) (L on colour rule). Fire-alarm box at Powell/Post.
- **Trees** (DataSF): Ficus microcarpa nitida 'Green Gem' is the dominant street tree — 4 on Powell's St. Francis side (S half of the block, ~7 m), 6 on Post at 300 Post (~4.4 m) + 2 magnolias & a tulip tree at the Apple corner, 12 on Geary's south side (~6 m), 12 on Geary Grant–Stockton; Brisbane box on Stockton Post–Sutter (~7 m); London planes + Ficus packed along Maiden Lane (~3 m); no street trees on the plaza-facing curbs of Geary/Stockton/Post (plaza trees belong to the plaza agent).
- **Other**: four "Hearts in SF" sculptures at the plaza corners ('SŌL' 2025 at Powell/Geary; 'Labyrinthine Heart' 2023 at Geary/Stockton); plaza-edge metal benches; inverted-U bike racks (10 at Powell/Post NW); 4 bollards on the Powell sidewalk at the SW corner; signal cabinets, USPS relay and collection boxes at most corners; sparse newspaper racks; drinking fountain, public toilet, taxi zones as listed in the JSON; holiday tree + ice rink Nov–Jan.
